## Overview

Professional work at Forvia for a Stellantis infotainment program. Details are limited by confidentiality;
this page summarises my role and the engineering challenges.

## My role

- Led a team of three engineers on the CEA workstream.
- Designed and validated the Bluetooth communication flow between the head unit and the phone app.
- Integrated cybersecurity mechanisms so data exchanged between the infotainment and IVI modules stays protected.
- Coordinated with cross-functional teams to meet OEM standards.

## What it taught me (and why it matters for AI)

Shipping software into a car means strict constraints, real-time behaviour and zero tolerance for flaky systems.
I now bring that discipline to AI: reproducible pipelines, measurable results and models that respect their hardware.
