## Overview

KnowTrack is a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) chatbot built by the AI team at Forvia for the whole company.
Instead of answering from memory alone, it retrieves relevant information from the company's data and uses it to
ground its answers, so everyone can use and understand the data held across the company.

It is built on Sinequa.
