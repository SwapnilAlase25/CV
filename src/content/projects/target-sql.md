## Questions explored

- How did order volume evolve over time and by state?
- What drives freight cost and delivery delays?
- How do customers pay, and in how many instalments?

## Approach

Pure SQL: joins across orders, customers, payments, items and sellers; window functions for trends;
aggregations for delivery performance.

## Outcome

Actionable recommendations on pricing strategy, shipping efficiency and customer satisfaction — a foundation case study from my MS program.
