## Problem

Do different customer groups really spend differently on Black Friday — or is it just noise?

## Approach

- Cleaned and explored the transaction dataset with Pandas.
- Used bootstrapped sampling to build sampling distributions of mean spend per segment.
- Applied the Central Limit Theorem to compute 90/95/99% confidence intervals.
- Checked where intervals overlap to decide which differences are statistically meaningful.

## Outcome

Clear, statistically validated recommendations on which segments to target — a foundation case study from my MS program.
